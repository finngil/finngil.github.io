## Reverse Engineered Music Stand Part
---
{% include image-gallery.html images="stand3dp.jpg" height="400" %}
<br>
When my the mechanism that fixed my music stand at a certain height broke, I figured out that the threads on the bolt were worn out. After discovering that the part was a standard M4 bolt, I used my own bolt and designed a quick thumb screw adaptor.
## Model Aircraft Stand
---
{% include image-gallery.html images="model3dp.jpg" height="400" %}
<br>

## Bird Feeder CAD Competition
---
{% include image-gallery.html images="bird.png" height="400" %}
<br>
I designed and modelled a bird feeder in CAD for a school competition that I entered and won. The design utilises an upcycled bottle that is filled with bird feed and inverted, the feed automatically dispenses through the holes.
## Room Design
---
{% include image-gallery.html images="room1.png" height="400" %}
<br>
When redecorating my bedroom, I had an idea for a custom desk combined with a flat-pack shelving unit. I measured the dimensions of my room and the existing shelving unit and modelled my idea in CAD, I then communicated my design with a local carpenter who worked with my design to build the desk.
