This part was designed as a sort of “holster” - it would be attached inside a bag in order to 
secure the devices for easy access and secure storage.

It was important to ensure the device slid freely inside the case, the geometry on the left hand
side of the case was designed to slide "around" protruding ports.

This part was a protective case that was printed with flexible material which reduced the risk of
damage from drops, and still allowed the buttons to be operated.

The flexible material used required a different tolerance from the hard shell cases to ensure the
device could still easily be placed inside the case, but without it sliding out.

## The Design Process
Each time I was commissioned to design a case, I sketched my idea with the client, took measurements
with calipers, worked out tolerances and modelled the case in CAD. After a couple of iterations, I
would reach a final design, each of which the technician was extremely satisfied with, he ordered the maximum
number of cases that would fit in the bag. 
---
Over the coming years, I have been paid to design
and produce different cases for increasingly more complex equipment. I believe that this experience is
extremely relevant to the engineering sector: consulting with a client, understanding their requirements,
before presenting a unique solution and working with them to finalise it.
